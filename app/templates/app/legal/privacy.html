Privacy Policy

How PrivyVote handles and protects user information

1. Information We Collect

PrivyVote collects limited information required for authentication, election participation, and system operation.

  • Account Information: Basic user details such as username, email, or profile-related data used in the system
  • Verification Information: Identity-related input used for voter verification through salted hashing
  • Voting Data: Encrypted ballot data and election participation records
  • Technical Data: Basic system and browser data required for platform operation and security monitoring

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information only for the core operation of the voting platform.

  • To authenticate users and verify voter eligibility
  • To prevent duplicate voting
  • To store encrypted ballots securely
  • To support election integrity and auditability
  • To maintain and improve system performance and reliability

3. Identity Protection

PrivyVote is designed to reduce direct exposure of sensitive identity information. Identity-related values used for voter verification are processed using salted hashing instead of being stored in plain readable form wherever applicable in the system design.

4. Vote Privacy

The privacy of each voter’s ballot is a core design goal of PrivyVote.

  • Votes are stored in encrypted form
  • Individual ballot choices are not intended to be exposed in plaintext
  • Vote data is separated from direct voter identity wherever possible in the system workflow
  • Only final aggregated election results are intended to be published

5. Encryption and Secure Processing

PrivyVote uses encryption-focused mechanisms to protect ballot confidentiality.

  • Encrypted ballots are used for vote storage
  • Homomorphic tallying logic is used for secure result computation
  • Only the final aggregate result is decrypted at the end of the election process
  • The system is designed to avoid storing plaintext vote choices during normal voting flow

6. Blockchain Auditability

PrivyVote uses blockchain support as an audit layer to help maintain integrity of election-related records. This helps reduce the risk of unauthorized modification after vote submission or result finalization.

7. Information Sharing

PrivyVote does not share personal information for unrelated commercial purposes.

  • Information may be viewed by authorized administrators only as required for platform management
  • Encrypted voting data may be processed as part of election operations
  • Information may be disclosed if required for academic review, demonstration, or lawful obligation

8. Data Retention

Data may be retained for the duration required to support election functionality, result validation, audit review, and project demonstration needs. Retention behavior may vary depending on system configuration.

9. User Rights

Users may request correction of account-related information where applicable.

  • Users may review basic profile information stored in their account
  • Users may request correction of inaccurate account data
  • Encrypted vote records and finalized election records may not be altered once submitted, in order to preserve election integrity

10. Limitations

PrivyVote is an academic project focused on secure electronic voting concepts. While privacy and security principles are built into the design, the platform should be understood within the scope of project implementation, testing, and demonstration.

11. Policy Updates

This privacy policy may be updated when the project design, implementation, or documentation changes. The latest version will always be shown on this page.

12. Contact

For questions about privacy or data handling, please contact us.
