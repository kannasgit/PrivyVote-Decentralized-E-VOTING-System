Why PrivyVote?

Designed for privacy, integrity, and secure digital election handling

Verified Access

Aadhaar or VID-based salted verification helps ensure only eligible users can vote.

Encrypted Ballots

Votes are stored in encrypted form to reduce exposure of individual ballot choices.

Audit Trail

Blockchain-backed records support election integrity and reduce tampering risk.

One-Time Tally

Final results are computed through homomorphic aggregation and revealed only after election closure.
